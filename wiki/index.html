How to Join the Server

Follow these simple steps to start playing on CrackedNetwork.

Server IP

crackednetwork.run.place

Port Java

25565 (Default)

Use 11111 if 25565 not working

Versi

1.8 - Latest Release

Steps:

  1. Open Minecraft (Java Edition)
  2. Click "Multiplayer" or "Play" → "Servers"
  3. Click "Add Server" or "Add External Server"
  4. Enter IP: crackednetwork.run.place
  5. Click "Done," then select the server and click "Join Server"
  6. Have fun playing!

How Is Our System Work?

We believe in transparency. Here is how our backend system processes your purchases and data.

  • Ranks System
    • You purchase a rank on our Website Store.
    • Payment is processed securely via CraftingStore/PayPal.
    • Our Plugin detects the successful payment instantly.
    • Permissions & Prefix are automatically applied to your account in-game.
    • You receive a confirmation message in-game and on Discord.
  • Coins System
    • Coins can be purchased on the Store or earned via Voting/Events.
    • Coins are stored in our central database linked to your UUID.
    • Use coins in /shop to buy items, keys, or cosmetics.
    • Purchases with coins are instant and non-refundable.
  • Punishment System
    • Staff receives a report via Discord or in-game.
    • Evidence is gathered and reviewed by the Staff Team.
    • If rules are broken, a ban/mute is logged into the system.
    • You can appeal bans on our Discord Server.
  • Data Security
    • We do not store credit card info (handled by PayPal/Stripe).
    • Passwords are encrypted and hashed.
    • Your data is never sold to third parties.
