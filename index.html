Steps :

How Is Our System Work?

We believe in transparency. Here is how our backend system processes your purchases and data.

  • Ranks System
    • (1) You purchase a rank on our Website Store.
    • (2) Payment is processed securely via CraftingStore/PayPal.
    • (3) Our Plugin detects the successful payment instantly.
    • (4) Permissions & Prefix are automatically applied to your account in-game.
    • (5) You receive a confirmation message in-game and on Discord.
  • Coins System
    • (1) Coins can be purchased on the Store or earned via Voting/Events.
    • (2) Coins are stored in our central database linked to your UUID.
    • (3) Use coins in /shop to buy items, keys, or cosmetics.
    • (4) Purchases with coins are instant and non-refundable.
  • Punishment System
    • (1) Staff receives a report via Discord or in-game.
    • (2) Evidence is gathered and reviewed by the Staff Team.
    • (3) If rules are broken, a ban/mute is logged into the system.
    • (4) You can appeal bans on our Discord Server.
  • Data Security
    • (1) We do not store credit card info (handled by PayPal/Stripe).
    • (2) Passwords are encrypted and hashed.
    • (3) Your data is never sold to third parties.
